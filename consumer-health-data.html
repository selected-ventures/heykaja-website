Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy applies to residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act), Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy. Kaja is a coaching app, not a health care provider. However, what you tell Kaja about emotional stress, your relationship or personal crises may count as "consumer health data" under these laws, so we treat it that way.

1. Consumer health data we collect

  • What you write or say in your quiz answers and conversations, which may reveal information about your emotional or mental well-being (for example stress, conflict, grief or crisis)
  • The communication patterns and next steps Kaja derives from it
  • Signals from our safety systems, for example that a message may point to an acute crisis or to violence

We do not collect precise location, biometric data or data from health devices.

2. Sources

We collect this data directly from you when you use the app. Kaja's safety and coaching systems then derive further information from it.

3. Why we collect and use it

  • To provide the coaching you asked for: analysis, conversations, next steps and check-ins
  • To detect crises and show you crisis resources
  • To keep the service secure and working
  • To improve the quality and safety of Kaja's replies – in the beta, also by having a small team review conversations (see section 6 of our Privacy Policy)

We collect and use consumer health data only with your consent, which you give in the app before your first free-text answer, and only as needed for these purposes.

4. Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with the service providers who process it on our behalf to run Kaja:

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud): hosting and database (stored in the European Union)
  • Anthropic: generating Kaja's replies
  • OpenAI: voice transcription, only if you use voice input

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes or for advertising. We disclose it otherwise only if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety in an emergency.

5. Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data (we never sell it), and access it, including a list of the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Have your consumer health data deleted, including in the app by deleting your account

To use these rights, email hello@heykaja.com with the subject "Consumer health data request". We will answer within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We will answer your appeal within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Attorney General of your state (in Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint).
