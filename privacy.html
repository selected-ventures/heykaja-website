Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Selected Ventures LLC ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the Kaja app and the website heykaja.com. Kaja is an AI coaching app for relationship dynamics. Because conversations about relationships are personal, we try to collect as little as possible and to be plain about what happens to it.

If you live in Washington, Nevada or another state with a consumer health data law, please also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • First name: the name you enter during onboarding, so Kaja can address you personally.
  • Quiz answers: your answers to the onboarding quiz (multiple-choice questions and two short free-text answers) and the pronoun you choose for your partner. We use them to create your communication profile and prepare your first conversation.
  • Conversations: the messages you send to Kaja and Kaja's replies, so Kaja can refer back to earlier topics and follow up on your progress.
  • Patterns and next steps: the communication pattern Kaja identifies and the steps it suggests, so it can support you across conversations and in check-ins.
  • Voice input (optional): if you use voice input, your recording is turned into text (see section 4). You can always type instead.
  • Age confirmation: your confirmation that you are 18 or older, and when you gave it.

Your conversations may include information about other people, such as your partner. Please share only what you need for your coaching. You do not need to give us anyone's full name.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • A random app user ID, created when you first open the app (we use anonymous sign-in, so you don't need an email address or password)
  • Device type, operating system version, app version, language and time zone
  • Crash reports and technical logs, so we can find and fix errors
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications
  • Usage analytics: which screens and features are used (for example, that the quiz was completed or the AI notice was shown). These contain no names and no conversation content

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use third-party advertising or attribution tools in the app.

2.3 Sign in with Apple or Google (optional)

If you choose to back up or restore your account with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier from that provider and, depending on your settings, an email address. We use it only to link your account and let you restore it.

2.4 Payments

Subscriptions are sold only through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment. We never see your card or bank details. We receive information about your subscription status (for example, active, in trial or expired) through our subscription service provider, RevenueCat.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the coaching: analyzing your quiz, running your conversations, suggesting next steps and remembering context
  • To send check-ins and reminders, if you have allowed notifications (you can turn them off at any time in your iPhone settings)
  • To keep you and others safe: automated systems look for signs of acute crisis, violence or that the user is a minor, so Kaja can show crisis resources or end a conversation
  • To manage your subscription and restore purchases
  • To keep the app working, secure and reliable, and to fix errors
  • To improve the quality and safety of Kaja's replies – in the beta, also by reviewing conversations (see section 6)
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Use

Kaja analyzes your quiz answers and messages automatically to identify communication patterns and tailor its coaching. This personalization is the service itself. It is not used to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for targeted advertising. We do not use your conversations to advertise to you.

4. AI processing

4.1 Conversations – Anthropic

Kaja's replies are generated by an AI language model from Anthropic PBC (San Francisco, USA). To create a reply, your messages, your first name, your quiz results and earlier context from your conversation are sent to Anthropic through its commercial API. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and does not use it to train its models. Anthropic may retain API data for a limited period under its own policies, for example to detect abuse.

4.2 Voice input – OpenAI

If you use voice input, your recording is sent through our servers to OpenAI (San Francisco, USA) to be transcribed. Under its API terms, OpenAI processes the audio on our behalf and does not use it to train its models. We do not keep the recording or the transcript on our servers. The text appears in your input field and becomes part of your conversation only when you send it. Until a recording has been transcribed successfully, it stays on your device so it isn't lost. It is removed once it's transcribed, when you discard it, or when you delete your account.

Kaja is an AI, not a person. The app reminds you of this regularly.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Kaja, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud): hosting, database, sign-in, usage analytics (Firebase Analytics), crash reports (Crashlytics), push notifications
  • Anthropic: generating Kaja's replies
  • OpenAI: voice transcription, only if you use voice input
  • RevenueCat: subscription status
  • Apple: App Store purchases, push notification delivery and, if you choose it, Sign in with Apple

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety in an emergency, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. If that happens, this policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. Review of conversations

Released app: nobody at Kaja reads your conversations. We have no tool for reviewing conversations in the released app. If we ever want to introduce one, we will ask for your consent first.

Beta (TestFlight): the beta runs on a separate test system. To improve Kaja's quality and safety, a small team may read conversations from the beta, including conversations where crisis or violence detection was triggered. We do not use beta conversations for marketing and do not share them with third parties for their own purposes. Beta accounts and conversations are not carried over to the released app – you start fresh there. If you don't want your beta conversations reviewed, email hello@heykaja.com or delete your beta account in the app.

7. Where your data is stored

Your account, quiz results and conversations are stored in the European Union, on Google Cloud (Firebase) servers. The following is processed in the United States: requests to the AI services (Anthropic, and OpenAI for voice input), usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Crashlytics). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Profile, quiz results and conversations: for as long as your account exists. You can delete a single conversation or your whole account in the app.
  • After you delete your account: your account, conversations, quiz results and subscription and notification data are permanently deleted within one hour. We do not keep backups of this data.
  • Technical logs: kept for 30 days. They do not contain conversation content.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): kept for 2 months. Aggregated reports that can no longer be linked to you may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): kept for about 90 days. They contain no names and no conversation content.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: delete your account in the app under Settings. This permanently deletes your account, conversations and quiz results.
  • Notifications: turn check-ins off at any time in your iPhone settings.
  • Voice input: optional. You can always type instead.
  • Access, correction, copy: email us to get a copy of your data or to correct it.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or emailing us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, such as the right to know, access, correct or delete your personal information, to get a portable copy, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising or profiling. We honor these requests for all users, whether or not a state law applies. To make a request, email hello@heykaja.com. We may need to verify that the account is yours. We answer within 45 days, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If your appeal is denied, you can contact your state Attorney General.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), restrict access to personal data to the people who need it, and keep the released app's systems separate from our test systems. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

11. Adults only

Kaja is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If Kaja learns during a conversation that a user is a minor, it ends coaching on that account. If you think a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. California notes

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the service. Our website and app do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because we don't track you across third-party sites in the first place.

13. Website

heykaja.com does not use advertising or analytics cookies. When you visit, our hosting and content delivery providers process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page securely. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The current version is always available at heykaja.com/privacy.html.
